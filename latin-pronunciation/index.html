How to pronounce the Latin of the Mass

Church Latin is pronounced the Roman way, much like Italian: c before e or i sounds like ch, and ae like a plain e. Classroom (classical) Latin rebuilds the speech of ancient Rome, while the Church kept the later, Italian-style sound used in Rome.

The table below sets out the rules that matter most at Mass.

The vowels

Each vowel has one clear sound and keeps it. None of them slides into a second sound the way English vowels do.

Where the stress falls

A word of two syllables is stressed on the first: Deus, pater. In longer words, missals and chant books print an accent over the stressed vowel, as in Dóminus and misericórdia, so you never have to guess.

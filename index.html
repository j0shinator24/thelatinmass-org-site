Where the money goes

This is a not-for-profit project. After Stripe processing fees and minimal operating costs, all subscription revenue will be split 50/50 between Mary Immaculate Annerley and St Joseph's Chapel (FSSP) at Nudgee College — both Latin Mass parishes in Brisbane, Australia.

A public transparency ledger of every disbursement will be published when subscriptions go live.

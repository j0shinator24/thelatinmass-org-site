The most common Latin words of the Mass

If you learn any Latin for Mass, start here. These are the words that appear most often in the Mass texts the app teaches, from the 1962 Missal. Each one is given with its English meaning and the number of times it appears.

Counts are the app's own tally across the Mass texts it teaches, the Order of Mass and selected Propers of the 1962 Missal, with every form of a word counted under its root. So Dómini, Dóminum and Dómine all count towards Dominus. The audio is AI-generated.

Why a short list goes a long way

A small number of words does most of the work in any language, and the Mass is no different. Forty words will not let you translate a Collect, but they will let you hear where you are: who is being addressed, who is speaking, and what is being asked.

Reading a list is one thing. Remembering it on Sunday is another, and the flashcards are for that. Try five on the home page, no account needed.
